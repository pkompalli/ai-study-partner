## Comparison Across All Three

| | BU Freshman | MBBS Intern | UPSC Aspirant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time horizon** | 18 days | 9 months | 12 months |
| **Energy constraint** | Not a factor | Post-shift fatigue | Burnout over long haul |
| **Unique challenge** | Fix 3 weak topics | 19 subjects + internship | Vastness + current affairs |
| **Study source** | App generates content | Marrow / PrepLadder | Standard books (Laxmikanth etc.) |
| **App's role** | Tutor + practice | Schedule + track + test | Schedule + integrate CA + test + re-plan |
| **Rest philosophy** | Not discussed | Realistic hour caps | Sundays off, explicit burnout warning |
| **Plan structure** | Weekly tactical | 3 phases, MCQ-heavy | 3 phases, subject rotation |
| **Unique feature** | Weak topic targeting | Working day vs day off split | Newspaper ↔ syllabus linking |
| **Tone** | Encouraging, light | Real, respectful of fatigue | Respectful of the marathon |

### Design Principles (consistent across all three)

1. **Feels like a conversation** — no step numbers, no progress bars, plain language throughout
2. **Every question earns visible value** — agent starts working while student answers the next question
3. **Transparent reasoning** — parentheticals explain WHY we're asking each thing
4. **Student feels heard** — system adapts language and plan based on their specific situation
5. **Agent does the heavy lifting** — web search for syllabi, structure extraction, plan generation all happen automatically
6. **Calendar offered as value delivery** — "want me to put these in your calendar?" not "give me access to your calendar"
7. **Realistic, not aspirational** — respects energy levels, includes rest, acknowledges that plans will change
